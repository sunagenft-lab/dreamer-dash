Why the board looks like this

DREAMER is 150 unique 1/1s: cover art by Sun, song by @johnny_olaleye (John O), launched Oct 9, 2026 on IMCollectibles.

The ladder only needs to be as tall as the lead. A holder's rung is simply the pile they hold: the current leader stands highest on the board, with the rungs above left as headroom. The collection's own climb lives on the side bar beside the board instead, so the moon stays what it really is: sold out, shared by everyone.

That is also why the race stays fair as it grows: a brand new holder starts on rung 1 next to everyone else, and anyone can climb, one mint at a time, all the way past the leader. Nobody has to reach the moon alone — you just have to be in the lead when the collection gets there.
